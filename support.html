Terms & Conditions

1. Reservations & Check-In

  • Reservations are confirmed upon receipt of a valid booking reference and, where applicable, payment.
  • Check-in is from 2:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability and may attract an additional fee.
  • Guests must present a valid government-issued ID or passport at check-in.
  • The minimum age to book and check in is 18 years.

2. Room Occupancy

  • Room rates are quoted per night in Kenyan Shillings (KES) and include the stated amenities.
  • Occupancy above the stated maximum for each room type may require an extra bed at an additional charge.
  • Children under 12 years stay free when sharing existing bedding with accompanying adults.

3. Guest Conduct

  • Smoking is prohibited in all indoor areas; designated outdoor smoking zones are provided.
  • Pets are not permitted, except for certified service animals.
  • Guests are liable for any damage caused to hotel property during their stay.
  • AUREXIS reserves the right to refuse service or terminate a stay for conduct that endangers guests or staff.

4. Liability

  • A safety deposit box is available in every room; AUREXIS is not liable for valuables left unattended elsewhere.
  • Personal belongings brought into the hotel are at the guest's own risk.
  • These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Kenya.

Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

  • Booking details: name, email address, phone number, and stay preferences provided during reservation.
  • Payment details are processed through secure, encrypted channels and are never stored in full on our systems.
  • Website usage data such as pages visited, collected only in aggregate to improve our services.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To confirm, manage and personalise your reservation and stay.
  • To send booking confirmations, receipts and service-related notifications.
  • With your consent, to share offers and promotions — you may opt out at any time.

3. Data Sharing & Security

  • We never sell your personal data. Information is shared only with payment processors and service providers strictly necessary to fulfil your booking.
  • All data is transmitted over 256-bit SSL encryption and stored on secured systems.

4. Your Rights

  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting info@aurexis.com.
  • We retain booking records only as long as required by Kenyan tax and hospitality regulations.

Payment Methods

AUREXIS accepts the following payment options. All transactions are processed securely — see our Secure Payment page to pay online.

  • Card Payment

    Visa, Mastercard and Amex — processed over 256-bit SSL encryption.

  • M-Pesa

    Pay instantly from your Safaricom line via an STK push prompt.

  • Bank Transfer

    Direct deposit to NCBA Bank Kenya — AUREXIS Hotel Ltd, 1234 5678 90 (SWIFT: NBAFKENX).

  • Pay at Desk

    Settle your bill at the front desk on arrival by cash or card.

All prices are quoted in Kenyan Shillings (KES) and include 16% VAT where applicable. A receipt is issued for every transaction.

Cancellation Policy

Free Cancellation

  • Cancellations made 48 hours or more before the scheduled check-in date are free of charge, and any payment is refunded in full.
  • Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.

Late Cancellation & No-Show

  • Cancellations made less than 48 hours before check-in attract a one-night charge.
  • Guests who do not arrive without notice (no-show) are charged one night, and the remainder of the booking is released.

Modifications

  • Date changes are free of charge subject to availability and any rate difference.
  • To cancel or modify a booking, contact our reservations team at bookings@aurexis.com or call +254 700 123 456.

Force Majeure

  • Where a stay cannot proceed due to circumstances beyond reasonable control, AUREXIS will offer a full refund or a credit voucher valid for 12 months.
